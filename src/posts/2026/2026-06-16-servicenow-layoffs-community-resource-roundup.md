Last week ServiceNow laid off hundreds of people, a ["three-figure" number of roles](https://www.hrkatha.com/news/servicenow-cuts-hundreds-of-jobs-as-company-cites-ai-efficiencies/) across solution consulting, sales, product marketing, and learning & development. [The CEO had said no layoffs](https://www.sandiegouniontribune.com/2026/06/12/servicenows-ceo-said-no-layoffs-then-fired-63-employees-in-san-diego/). Then 63 people in San Diego alone were let go.

It's rough. But the ServiceNow community did what it always does: **showed up**.

Within hours, people were posting opportunities, offering free resume reviews, opening their networks, and sharing job boards. I spent yesterday collecting everything I could find. Here it all is in one place.

If you were affected. I'm here to help. Reach out and I'll try to help.

## Community Hubs & Job Boards

**Open Service Community (OSC)** [Job board](https://openservicecommunity.com/jobs.html) that's curated, allows anonymous searches, and has support built in. OSC also holds weekly calls **Tuesday evenings** and **Friday mornings** for anyone who needs community or direction. Multiple people (Matt Beran, Sean McClean) are pointing folks here.

**Jobs n Career Success Network** [Dawn C. Simmons's](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dawncsimmons_servicenow-layoffs-careertransition-ugcPost-7471008891087269889-9smO/) network of **75,000+ recruiters globally**. [Join here](https://www.linkedin.com/groups/2079/). The mission: if you have a job opening, share it. If you need a job, ask for help. If you can encourage someone, do it today.
