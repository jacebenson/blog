# LinkedIn Connection Template — Jace's Boilerplate

```
Hey [Name],

[Personal note — 1-2 sentences about their work, background, a post
 of theirs, or something specific to their company/role.]

Since we're connecting, wanted to share two things from my corner of the ServiceNow world:

I run thenulledge.com.  A set of free virtual conferences around ServiceNow and ServiceNow-adjacent content. We're always looking for speakers and sponsors if that's ever of interest.

Also, here's a page about me and what I do, in case any of it's useful: https://jace.pro/about/

No pressure on either.  I just wanted to put them on your radar.
```

---

## Why this works

The template gives them two things:

1. **Null Edge** — a specific, actionable offer (attend, speak, sponsor)
2. **Your about page** — a passive, zero-pressure link where they can discover your background, the Lombardo award, AI in a Box, and all your other projects on their own terms

No product pitch. No "let me tell you about my tool." Just "here's who I am if you're curious."

---

## Per-Persona Guide

| Persona | Personal note angle | Include Null Edge? | Include about page? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Customer Developer** | Their platform work, projects, contributions | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Both fit naturally |
| **Customer Management/CEO** | Their company's ServiceNow journey or challenges | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Null Edge as industry event, about page is just context |
| **Partner Developer** | Their product, integrations, tech work | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Both relevant |
| **Partner Management/CEO** | Partnership model, customers, growth | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Lead with Null Edge sponsorship |
| **ServiceNow Developer** | Platform work, community involvement | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | About page is safer than explicitly naming AI in a Box |
| **ServiceNow Sales/AD** | Their region, customers, background (e.g., SAP) | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Null Edge for their customers, about page if they're curious |
| **ServiceNow Exec** | Leadership, vision, platform impact | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Keep it high-level. About page lets them dig deeper if they want |

---

## Quick Rules

1. **Always start with the personal note.** That's what makes this human.
2. **Null Edge stays in for everyone.** It's a community event, not a product — zero risk.
3. **The about page replaces every product mention.** It covers AI in a Box, your awards, your history — all without you having to pitch any of it.
4. **If it feels too long, drop the about page line.** Null Edge is the stronger, more generous offer in most cases.
5. **The about page line never reads as a sales pitch** because you're not selling anything — you're just saying "here's who I am."
